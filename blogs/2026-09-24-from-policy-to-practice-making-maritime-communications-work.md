By Mario Maniewicz, Director, ITU Radiocommunication Bureau Every day, ships rely on radiocommunications to navigate safely, stay connected, and call for help when lives are at risk. Making these systems work across borders takes more than technology. It takes internationally agreed rules and the will to put them into practice.
